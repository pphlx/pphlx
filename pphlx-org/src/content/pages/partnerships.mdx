**Let's improve the web, together!**

Trusted by developers worldwide, PPHLX is the template compilation framework for building modern monolithic websites like blogs, marketing, e-commerce, and native web apps.

## Sponsorship

The easiest way to [partner with PPHLX](#how-to-partner-with-pphlx) is to become a sponsor! Your financial contributions support [the community behind the open source project](#community-maintained) for ongoing maintenance and development.
<Note>
	**To sponsor:** visit [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/pphlx) or [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/pphlx) and choose from a list of contribution tiers or a custom amount. Thank you for supporting PPHLX!
</Note>

We recognize some levels of sponsorship with a logo and/or link on PPHLX sites such as [our marketing homepage](/), [GitHub README](https://github.com/pphlx/pphlx), and [docs website](https://pphlx.org/docs/). We are always happy to work directly with larger sponsors ($2000+ per month) to create a custom sponsorship package.

## Community maintained

Thanks to our partners and sponsorships, we have been able to disperse open source sponsorships to PPHLX’s community contributors, which include **our core maintainers**.

Sponsorships have paid for community maintainers to lead PPHLX’s documentation efforts and manage translations, develop documentation themes, add essential features such as compiler optimization and type-safe environment variables, publish our monthly newsletter, and so much more!

Learn more about [how PPHLX's open source funding model works](/blog/supporting-the-future-of-pphlx/), and all of the parts of the project that depend on it!

## How to partner with PPHLX

PPHLX maintains relationships with companies, individuals, content creators, and other open source projects. There are limited opportunities to become a "Featured Partner" in some areas of the project.

Reach out to [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) to explore activities such as:

- [Co-marketing](#co-marketing)
- Building or funding [feature development](#development)
- Supporting [Documentation Templates](#documentation-templates)
- Becoming an [Agency Partner](#agency-partners)
- Sponsoring [a featured theme](#theme-sponsors)
- Inviting us on [podcasts or live streams](#podcasts-and-live-streams)
- Creating [video content](#video-content)
- Submitting [third-party guides](#third-party-guides) to our docs site
- Funding [community events](#community-events)
- Advertising in our [newsletter](#newsletter-advertising)
- Listing special [items in our swag shop](#sponsored-swag)

### Co-marketing

We can partner to amplify each others’ message, content, or resources to the benefit of both of our communities.

If you create or build something with or featuring PPHLX, we’re happy to share it! This could take the form of a social media post or boost, a blog post announcing our joint feature, or placement in our newsletter.

For larger coordinated efforts, reach out to us to discuss a potential partnership with a financial arrangement.

<Note>
	**To co-market:** email [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) a brief summary of what you want us to amplify, where you want us to amplify it, and how it helps PPHLX users.
</Note>

### Development

PPHLX stays lightweight, environmentally friendly, and blazingly fast thanks to a focused set of core features with minimal dependencies. That's why PPHLX's template ecosystem is so powerful!

**[Creating a PPHLX theme or template](https://pphlx.org/docs/en/reference/themes-reference/) for your product or service is a great way to make it easy for people to build monolithic apps, and grows the entire ecosystem!**

Don't have the time or expertise to build a PPHLX template for your company or product? You can partner with us to **directly fund development of a template**. This allows PPHLX to prioritize the functionality you need, and create a plug-and-play way for projects to use your service.

Is PPHLX missing a core feature that would allow you to use or migrate to it? As an open-source project, we welcome community contributors! Check out our GitHub repository and submit a proposal for missing features that you'd like to see added. If accepted, you are welcome to build it yourself with support and oversight of a core maintainer.

**We also work with partners who are able to fund the development of these core features, so get in touch!**

<Note>
	**To directly fund feature development:** email [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) with a brief summary of who you are, and the feature or template you need.
</Note>

### Documentation Templates

Our documentation templates are designed to compile lightweight, performant, accessible docs pages with a healthy ecosystem of components. Used by monolithic projects worldwide, our templates are a perfect docs-as-code solution for projects of any size.

Supporting our documentation maintenance and development is a great way to contribute to high-quality, low-footprint resources. With additional integrations, our docs templates can power your entire web presence, including marketing pages and a blog!

Using PPHLX for your docs? Consider supporting its ongoing maintenance and development through an Open Collective sponsorship, or reach out to us for additional partnership opportunities.

<Note>
	**To find out about documentation partnership opportunities:** email [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) with a brief summary of you and/or your project, and how you'd like to collaborate.
</Note>

### Third-party guides

PPHLX is proud of its commitment to documentation, and developers love our docs! Developers rely heavily on high-quality documentation and written resources when they build for the web. When they don't get it, they move on and choose something else.

PPHLX accepts third-party guides for installing, configuring, and integrating services with PPHLX (e.g. content management systems, deploy hosts, storage solutions) on a case-by-case basis. If accepted, our docs team will work with you to ensure a high quality, polished product up to the standard of our own documentation. These guides let PPHLX users know that your product or service can be used with PPHLX, and shows them exactly how to get started!

Please be advised that these documents can require extensive writing, testing, and editing to produce content that meets our documentation standards. Therefore, we may not always have the available resources to review and accept submissions. To help us include yours, you can **directly fund our docs team** to work with you on guides about using your product or service with PPHLX.

<Note>
	**To propose a guide or fund additional docs work:** email [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) with your service, timeline, and resources.
</Note>

### Community Events

PPHLX is nothing without the community behind it! We would love to better support events, meetup groups, conferences, and the people who organize and represent PPHLX at them.

We also occasionally plan official in-person events ourselves, such as [PPHLX Together](/blog/pphlx-together/) and launch events like the [PPHLX Solstice Festival](/blog/solstice-festival/).

Sponsorship can help send PPHLX community maintainers to speak at conferences, fund a booth or swag items for attendees, or make an entire event possible!

<Note>
	**To help with creating, organizing, or sponsoring an event:** email [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) with a proposal, or to see which events we are currently trying to attend.
</Note>

### Newsletter Advertising

PPHLX's newsletter "The PPHLX Post" reaches thousands of people each month with a roundup of the latest news, releases, and community showcase items. This is a completely community-funded initiative that keeps people informed and provides the latest tips and tools to help people get the most out of PPHLX.

PPHLX's newsletter is delivered directly to subscribers' inboxes with a consistent 55-65% open rate, and is permanently available in a web archive. Each month, we suggest articles, videos, and developer tools related to working with PPHLX. There are opportunities to feature your content, include an advertisement, or sponsor the month's newsletter or full monthly blog post so that our audience stays educated and informed.

<Note>
	**To place an advertisement or sponsored content in The PPHLX Post:** email [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) with a description of your related content or service.
</Note>

### Sponsored Swag

PPHLX's official swag shop [PPHLXMart](https://shop.pphlx.org/) is a community initiative to provide our community with opportunities to show off their PPHLX pride! All shop maintenance, from product design to order fulfillment, is supported by open source funds, and all profits are reinvested in the project.

We regularly offer special product lines for seasonal events or other promotions. Your sponsorship can help support the design of new products and the maintenance of the store itself. Consider sponsoring a special item! Co-branded merch? Providing a special discount? Let us know if you are interested in getting PPHLX swag into people's hands!

<Note>
	**To support the efforts of the PPHLX store:** email [partner@pphlx.org](mailto:partner@pphlx.org) with your idea for creating or sponsoring merch promotions.
</Note>
